# Home (/)

Scope: homepage, Persuade mode. Audience: SMB owners in EU/US with a business problem; secondary agencies. Action: package inquiry, priced stack, free audit. Proof: live concept builds with measured Lighthouse scores, founder record. Constraints: Lighthouse 90+ mobile and desktop, CLS ~0, reduced-motion static fallbacks, keyboard-complete, 375px up. Hero background video stays (user decision).

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Built pixel by pixel." Every key moment shows pixel blocks from the Matterpixel M (blue #2C4BFF, magenta #FF2E93) assembling into finished work. Refuses the category default of a centred headline over a row of equal service cards on a light template.

OWN-WORLD: near-black #0A0B10 ground with an 8px square-pixel texture at very low opacity; blue and magenta only as light, edges and pixel blocks, never as full-bleed fills (no blue band, no pink band). Inter Tight bold sentence-case headings, left-aligned, each H2 under a small-caps eyebrow with a magenta square dot. 1200px container, 120/72px section rhythm. Only two light sections: Build your stack and FAQ.

STORY: the visitor sees a real site assemble and score 100, names their problem, sees the package/price/proof, sees four live builds and the founder, prices a stack, then trusts the process and models, and acts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: two columns at desktop. Left: eyebrow, H1 "We build websites and AI systems that win customers and save hours.", subline, Get a Free Audit (primary) + View Projects, then a small monochrome tech row and trust badges. Right: ShopSphere browser frame assembling from pixel blocks over ~1.2s, then a Lighthouse badge counting 0→100; hover replays. Existing video stays dimmed behind. Mobile: mockup below the CTAs.

FORM: pinned by the user's brief. seed: none (pinned) — "one cohesive, premium, memorable page built around a single motif: 'Built pixel by pixel' — pixel blocks from the Matterpixel M mark (electric blue #2C4BFF, magenta #FF2E93) assembling into finished work", "dark base (#0A0B10) for the whole page". Signature interaction: pixel assembly (hero mockup, panel switches, section entrances as pixel-dissolve masks, stack pixels flying to the summary, the final pixel M).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
